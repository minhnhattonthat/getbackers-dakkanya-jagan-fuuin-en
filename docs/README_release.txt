How to patch
  Use any BPS patcher (Flips, beat, or Rom Patcher JS) with {patch}.
  Start a NEW GAME after patching; savestates made with the Japanese ROM will not work (save files do).

What is translated
  All story dialogue, choices, battle cut-ins and battle messages, speaker names, menus (status, party, items,
  equipment, options, sound test, save, act select), item and equipment names and descriptions, enemy names and
  database text, the staff credits, the title screen logo text and copyright line, the act title cards, the battle
  command, party, item and skill panels, the opening demo's name captions and title cards, and the shop signs
  in the town maps (Fortune-Teller Alley, the Honky Tonk street, the aquarium).
  English text uses a narrow 6-pixel font, two letters per original 12-pixel glyph; long messages continue in an
  extra text box. The ROM is 32 MiB after patching (the battle panels needed room).

Known issues
  - The katakana reading above the GetBackers logo was removed rather than replaced (the logo already says it).
  - The takoyaki shop sign "Run" on the Honky Tonk street keeps only its octopus picture (its letters share
    tiles with the rest of the sign, so no English fits there). A few tiny dot-patterns on posters and narrow
    vertical signs are left as they are.
  - Some Japanese was transcribed from the game's font by eye; a rare misread kanji may have shaped a line.

Tested in MesenCE 2.2.1 (cold boot, story, field, menus, battles).
